OCP documentation
==================

**Open Cryptanalysis Platform (OCP)** is an open-source platform for automated
cryptanalysis of symmetric-key primitives. It provides a unified representation for
block ciphers, permutations, and stream ciphers as round- and layer-structured
compositions of variables and operators. From this single representation, OCP
automatically generates executable Python/C/Verilog implementations, produces structural
visualization figures, and constructs MILP and SAT models and invokes external
solvers for cryptanalysis, including differential cryptanalysis and linear
cryptanalysis.
